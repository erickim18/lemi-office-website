About

A private boutique salon inside The Parlor on Madison Avenue, founded by Lena Yeo.

LEMI Office was founded with the vision of creating a transformation in every client, by helping them discover their unique beauty through new styles.

Our approach to beauty is more than simply cutting and styling hair. It’s about creating special moments in the lives of each client.

LEMI aims to combine the latest hair trends with personalized styling to bring out the individuality and confidence of every client.

We offer a full range of services, including hair styling, hair care, scalp treatments, and trend-led coloring.

Communication with clients is key. Our salon strives to deeply understand each individual’s lifestyle and preferences.
