At LEMI, we believe healthy hair begins at the scalp. A head spa is time set aside for it: a slow, focused treatment for the skin your hair grows from, not just the lengths you see in the mirror.

## The four steps

### 01 · Cleanse

A deep cleanse lifts away excess oil, product buildup, and impurities.

### 02 · Massage

Scalp massage releases tension and gives you a moment to slow down.

### 03 · Treat

Hydrating care restores balance to a scalp that feels dry, sensitive, or tired.

### 04 · Rest

We finish with relaxation care, for healthier-looking hair and scalp.

## Who it is for

- A scalp that feels dry, sensitive, or tired
- Hair that feels weighed down by oil or product buildup
- Anyone who wants to release tension and slow down
- Anyone who simply wants an hour that is only theirs

## How to fit it in

Our hydration head spa takes about 60 minutes. Add a blowout to leave ready for the day, or pair a head spa with your next haircut. The best results come with regular care, which is why we also offer packages of three sessions. See the [Head Spa menu](head-spa.html) for details and prices.
