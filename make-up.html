Your artist

Regina Choi

Regina began her career as a professional makeup artist in South Korea and is now based in New York, where she works as both a hairstylist and makeup artist.

Regina is dedicated to discovering and enhancing each client’s unique beauty. She believes that true beauty is individual and should never be confined to a single standard.

Her thoughtful attention to detail and keen eye allow her to create personalized looks that complement each person’s natural features, personality, and lifestyle.

If you’re ready to discover your own signature style and bring out your best self, book an appointment with Regina.
